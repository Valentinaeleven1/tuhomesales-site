# Raspberry Pi Drone Payload Checklist

## Purpose
A fast-build payload for drone-assisted signal search. This kit can be loaded with your rescue signal software and sent to drone operators.

## Recommended components
- Raspberry Pi 4 Model B (4GB) or Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W
- Raspberry Pi power supply or 5V USB power bank
- USB GPS receiver
- Signal sensor module:
  - RTL-SDR dongle for radio scanning, or
  - USB Bluetooth/Wi-Fi adapter for beacon detection
- MicroSD card (32GB or larger) with Raspberry Pi OS
- Compact case or enclosure
- Lightweight mount, straps, or bracket for drone attachment
- Cable ties and padding for secure mounting

## Suggested optional items
- Small USB hub (if more than one USB device is needed)
- External antenna for the signal receiver if needed
- Portable charger or spare batteries
- Weather protection enclosure if flying in dusty or damp conditions

## Notes
- Keep the total payload weight under the drone’s maximum recommended payload.
- Balance the unit on the drone and secure it tightly.
- Validate the setup before sending it into the field.
